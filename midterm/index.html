I build things, make things, and move people.

I'm Andy, a creative technologist, musician, and designer currently studying Creative Technology & Design at CU Boulder. During the day, I work as a Salesforce Developer and AI Specialist. At night, I'm ONDY, DJing sets built around energy and connection.

In between, I'm capturing moments through photography and video, designing interfaces, and figuring out how design and technology can feel more human. Everything I do comes back to the same question: how do we create things that work well and feel real?

Work

What I Build

I'm a Salesforce Developer and AI Specialist at CareerWise USA. I solve real business problems by understanding users first. My background in UI/UX design gives me a unique perspective. I design and code with the user in mind, which means building automation that makes sense, workflows that don't get in the way, and systems that feel intuitive instead of frustrating.

My Approach

I focus on making things that work and making them maintainable. That means clean code, thoughtful automation, and always asking whether a tool actually solves the problem or just looks impressive. Coming from both the design and development side, I care about building systems people can actually use.

Creative

Design, Photo, Video

I'm drawn to visual storytelling, whether that's designing an interface, shooting a photo series, or editing a video. As a CTD student, I care about how things look, but I care more about how they feel and how people move through them. Good design isn't just aesthetics. It's communication, rhythm, and intention.

Why I Make Things

My creative work lives at the intersection of technical thinking and artistic curiosity. I want to build experiences that feel intentional, where the layout, rhythm, and mood all work together. Whether it's a photo, an interface, or a video, I want people to feel something, not just see something.

Music

ONDY (My DJ Project)

ONDY is how I perform as a DJ. I play high-energy sets built around movement, intensity, and crowd connection. Dubstep, trap, DnB, UKG, bass house, and genre-fluid bass music. DJing is experience design in real time. You're reading the room, shaping energy, and creating moments people feel together. It's one of the clearest ways I express what I care about: making people feel something.

Sound & Style

I play bass-heavy, high-energy music that moves. My sets are built around momentum and emotional impact, reading the crowd and responding in real time. If you've been to a show, you know what I mean.
