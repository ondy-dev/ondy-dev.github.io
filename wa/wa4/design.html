Design and Media

I am pursuing a B.S. in Creative Technology & Design at the University of Colorado Boulder. My long-term goal is to find meaningful intersections between my technical background in software development and AI, and my creative interests in music and performance.

I am especially interested in how thoughtful design can connect these worlds to create products and experiences that are both technically strong and emotionally engaging. I am still early in my degree, but I am excited about the creative and technical work ahead. Stay tuned for more projects as my skills continue to grow.

Design focus and interests

  • UX and UI design with an emphasis on clarity and structure
  • Creative technology that blends design, code, and interaction
  • Designing systems and experiences, not just visual surfaces
  • Exploring how music, technology, and design can intersect

Selected project

Design Foundations Final Project: Monument Design Challenge

One of my first major design projects was my final project for Design Foundations, where my team was tasked with reimagining a school-related website experience. I spearheaded the design process and led the interface and layout work in Figma.

My role focused on organizing content, defining page hierarchy, and creating a clear and usable structure before any visual styling decisions were finalized. This project helped solidify my interest in UX design and design systems.
