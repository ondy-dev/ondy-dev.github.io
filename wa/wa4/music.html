Music and Performance

ONDY is my high-energy DJ performance persona. Through ONDY, I focus on bass-forward, crowd-driven sets designed to create movement, intensity, and shared moments. DJing is where my interest in experience design comes alive in real time.

Artist Overview: ONDY

ONDY is a bass-first DJ project built around high-impact live energy. My sets blend dubstep, trap, DnB, UKG, and genre-fluid bass music, with the goal of shaking walls and keeping crowds moving.

  • Genres: Dubstep, Trap, DnB, UKG, Bass House, genre-fluid sets
  • Style: High-energy, aggressive pacing, crowd-responsive
  • Mindset: Genre-fluid chaos with intention
