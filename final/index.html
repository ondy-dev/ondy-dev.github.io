Scream to Control Volume

The World's Worst Volume Control Interaction

Normal volume sliders are silent, discreet, and instant. This one is none of those things. You control the volume by making sustained noise into your microphone. The louder you are, the higher the volume. Stop making noise, and it resets. Ideal for libraries, quiet offices, and any other place where you'd rather not cause a scene.

Grant microphone access below to begin your suffering.

Your microphone audio is never recorded or sent anywhere. All processing happens locally in your browser.
