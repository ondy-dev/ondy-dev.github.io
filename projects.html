Tomorrow · text game · CU Boulder · Oct 2026ClassShipped

A short text game about one day, regret and letting go

You relive one Tuesday. It stops at seven moments where Nell could have done something different: one change per day, the day resets, and what she learned survives. I built the engine myself, which I enjoyed more than I expected: the story is data in one file, the rules are a few small classes, a script generates the decision map, and a test plays the whole game without a browser. Sixteen simulated playthroughs changed the story, from the safety replies to a sixth fix.

Asian Unity at CU Boulder · Co-PR and Creative Lead · 2026ClubShipped

A campus identity that behaves like a broadcast

I run the visual side of Asian Unity with my co-PR: logo, graphics, social, photo, video and motion. This year's theme is Y2K Tokyo pop, and the teaser is the piece I'd show first if you asked me what my degree is about. It flips through CRT static like someone changing channels, lands on an "incoming transmission" message, then a music drop and the logo reveal. In one 30-day period the account reached 140K Instagram views, 2.5K interactions and 200 new followers. The photography this year is mine.

3D narrative animation · CU Boulder · Spring 2026ClassShipped

A 45-second short from storyboard to sound

A Five Nights at Freddy's-inspired piece for an image class: camcorder and surveillance points of view, keyframed animatronic performance, UV-mapped TV screens, pixel art from Figma, then compositing and sound design in Premiere. It was the first time I'd taken something from thumbnails all the way to a finished cut, and the sound design is the part I'd do again tomorrow.

AndyGetAJobChallenge · private web app · Oct 2026PersonalShipped

A job-search tool I built for myself

I was applying to a lot of roles, so I built the tool I wanted: React and TypeScript, Postgres with row-level security, one serverless function, and a scheduled Claude Code routine that finds postings, scores them and tailors a resume from a JSON base. Its tokens file is the one this site uses. The card's rules came out of four review rounds: text before numbers, and never a judgment without its evidence. It's private, because it holds my applications.
