# Tomorrow — Story Bible & Systems

> Every change is a "what if." The only way to move on is to live the day
> exactly as it happened, choosing it this time, knowing everything you know.

**Play:** `/tomorrow/` · **Code:** `game.js` (the day is `SEGS`, the changes are `BRANCH`)

Inspired by Sam Barlow's *Aisle*: one input changes everything, and meaning builds up across short replays.

---

## The rule

You relive the same Tuesday. It plays out the way it happened and **stops at seven moments** where Nell could have done something different.

- **Press ENTER** to let the moment happen. The day goes on as it did.
- **Type something** to change it. The rest of the day branches from there, then night, then Tuesday again.

You get **one change per day**. `LOOK`, `THINK`, and `HELP` never change anything.

**The day resets. Your knowledge doesn't.** What you've learned, tried, and seen is saved in the browser.

## The seven moments

| # | Time | The moment | What you could do differently |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 7:14 AM | "Maya never replied last night." | `look at phone`, `look at photos`, `remember`, `stay in bed`, `go to her house`, `go to the tower` / `text maya`, `ask mom`, `breathe` |
| 2 | Kitchen | Mom at the screen door: "Lourdes came by the pharmacy…" | `ask mom` |
| 3 | 11:40 AM | Theo: "She didn't tell you." Somebody laughs. | `breathe`, `talk to theo` |
| 4 | 3:15 PM | "She might be up there…" | `go to the tower` |
| 5 | 6:30 PM | "Say something." | `tell her i love her`, `ask her to stay`, `say goodbye` / `hug` / `sorry` |
| 6 | Porch | "You could knock." | `knock` / `follow` / `sorry` |
| 7 | 8:40 PM | "You could still make it." | `go to the station`, `remember` |

Trying something in the wrong place doesn't use up the day:
- too early → *"Not yet."* (or *"She isn't here. Not yet."*)
- too late → *"That moment's already gone. Today, anyway."*

Typing the original choice (for example `fine then go` at moment 5) counts as letting it happen.

---

## The shape of the game

**Day 1:** you can type at every moment, but nothing changes:
> *You mean to say "i love you." You lift your hand. You put it down.*

**Middle days:** each change teaches something or tries to fix something. None of them end the loop.

**The way out:** once Nell has tried **3 different fixes**, the night ends with *"Something about the dark feels different tonight. Thinner."* After that, letting all seven moments go by plays the day with **the same events and the same words**, told honestly for the first time. Then Wednesday. `get up` → *You do.*

Letting everything happen **before** that doesn't work. She isn't letting go yet:
> *At every one of them, you stopped and looked. It feels like holding your breath.*

Morning hints once the way out is open: *"Every one except the first" → "What if you didn't change anything?" → "Let every chance go by."*

## Repetition

- Moments you've let happen twice get **shorter on replay** (*"The cold eggs. Mom, asking the coffee maker…"*). The last day is always told in full.
- The opening line changes once you learn the truth: *"Maya never replied last night. That's the story, anyway."*
- An ENTER pressed in the first ~0.7 seconds after a moment appears is ignored, so turning pages can't skip a moment by accident.

## The unreliable narrator

Each distorted moment is told one of three ways:

| | When | Diner example |
|---|---|---|
| **felt** | Nell doesn't know the truth yet | *The whole diner turns to look at you, like sunflowers.* (the text wobbles on screen) |
| **heal** | she's learned the truth, but it still feels the old way | *At the TV. You know that now. It still feels like it's at you.* |
| **clear** | only on the way-out day | *It's at the TV. It was always at the TV.* |

| How Nell remembers it | What happened | Learned from |
|---|---|---|
| "Maya never replied." | Maya texted at 11:52 PM. Nell left it on Read. | `look at phone` (after learning she tried) |
| The diner laughed at her. | Truckers and a game show. The laugh was at the TV. | `breathe` / `talk to theo` |
| Everybody knew. | Theo found out at 6 AM and dreaded the booth. | `breathe` / `talk to theo` |
| *"That's just you. That's fine."* (mean laugh) | Crying: *"If you'd asked, I would have stayed."* | `go to the tower` |
| She never tried to tell her. | Thursday at the tower; Nell made a joke. | Theo, the tower, `remember` |

## The changes

**Learning** (one moment changes, then *"the rest of Tuesday goes the way Tuesday goes"*): phone, photos, remember, mom, house, breathe / talk to Theo.
**Feelings** unlock from the sketchbook (`go to her house`), or from the photos + remembering the tower.

**Fixing** (need 3 of 5):

| Fix | Where | Needs | Why it isn't enough |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Peace** | tower (1 or 4), goodbye (5) | — | Good "the way a photograph is good." |
| **Keep** | ask her to stay (5) | Knowing why she's going | She stays. The streets get shorter. |
| **Confess** | tell her (5) | Feelings | She says it back. She still goes. |
| **Sorry** | knock (6) | — | "Sorry doesn't make it un-happen. It just makes it happen nicer." |
| **Leave** | the bus (7) | — | The road circles the town like water around a drain. |

Repeating a fix you've already done: *"Why does it sound like you practiced that?"* It counts for nothing.

A typical playthrough is about 7–9 days.

## Characters

### Nell (Eleanor) — "you"
Seventeen and has never lived anywhere but Calder. Funny as a defense; every hard moment gets a joke. She has never said the word *gay* about herself, not even in her head. Maya is the one place she doesn't perform. What scares her isn't Maya leaving, it's change. Calder is an eleven-street loop, and she was already living in it.
**Shows it by:** making jokes when she should listen, putting the phone face down, never asking for her jacket back.

### Maya Reyes
Charcoal on her thumb, always. Got into an art school in Chicago with real money and kept it secret, **because she knew that if Nell asked her to stay, she would.** She has worn Nell's jean jacket since March and drawn Nell for a year. She tried to tell her Thursday and lost her nerve at a joke.
**Voice:** short, teasing ("Nellie"), honest when cornered.

### Mom (Denise)
Pharmacy tech. Loves Nell in practical ways: cold eggs, a kiss on the head she doesn't usually give. She talks to the coffee maker instead of to you. *"Friends who aren't—"* She never finishes it.

### Theo Park
Works at Dot's Diner. Nervous, sweet, talks fast, pours coffee nobody ordered. Found out at 6 AM and dreaded the booth all morning.

### Mrs. Reyes (Lourdes)
Hugs too hard, smells like onions and Jergens. She can't keep a secret, which is how the whole town found out. Calls Nell *mija*.

### Calder
Eleven streets: the Dollar General, the Baptist church, the feed store, Dot's, and the water tower (the CALDER lettering they repainted badly at sixteen). The time loop is the town made literal.
