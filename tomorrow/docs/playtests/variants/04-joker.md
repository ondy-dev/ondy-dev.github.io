# Nell, variant 04: "joker" (anxiety 6/10)

Played on the real game with the harness, one input at a time. 67 inputs in total. Ending: **the true ending (with the truth), after 7 Tuesdays.** The game's own end card said: "7 Tuesdays. There were other ways to spend them."

## Who I am

> It's 2 AM and I've done eleven minutes on whether the stain is Ohio. It isn't Ohio. Maya says Ohio. Said. Says. She's on a bus, so I guess it's present tense somewhere in Indiana. That's a joke. See? I'm fine. If it's funny it doesn't count.

| **Anxiety** | 6/10. It never looks like panic. It looks like a punchline that lands half a second too fast. The worse it gets, the better the bits get. |

## Her chains

1. **"She said I made a joke."**
   *Which joke? I make forty a day. Thursday, at the tower. Hotel California. She asked what I'd do if she left, and I didn't look at her face. If I'd looked, I'd have known. But I only joke when I'm scared. So I was scared Thursday. Of what?*
   - "She said I made a joke": existing (theo / breathe, `tried_to_tell`).
   - Thursday, the joke, "didn't look at her face": existing (remember, `[if tried_to_tell]`).
   - **"Scared of what?"**: NEW link. This is her road to `feelings`, and she never got there. She never looked at photos or went to the house. The joker's door to her feelings is the joke itself, and the game has no step from "I joke when I'm scared" to "I was scared because I love her".

2. **"If she'd just stayed, I'd be fine."**
   *She stayed, and she stopped drawing. "Chicago was a stupid idea anyway": that's my voice. So I'm the one who'd have talked her out of it, one bit at a time.*
   - Existing (keep fix + night). It landed exactly as designed.

3. **"Nobody leaves Calder."**
   *Fine, then I'll leave. The bus goes in circles. So my joke was true. So it wasn't a joke.*
   - Bus: existing (station). **The callback to her own line is NEW.** The game sets it up and doesn't land it.

4. **"Who laughed? I'll make them sorry."**
   *Turn around, and it's the TV. A man winning a boat.*
   - The truth is existing (theo). **The way in is NEW.** The anger route plays the Theo text ("You make yourself look at him"), which describes the opposite of what she did.

5. **"Maya never replied."**
   *She never doesn't reply. Did I even… no. Phone stays face down.*
   - Existing (phone + `tried_to_tell`, which teaches `read`). She knew enough to unlock it from Tuesday 3 on and never once picked up the phone. That's in character, so the true-ending night stayed vague: "About everything she was trying to hand you." Not a bug, but it means a joker can finish without ever facing the text or her feelings.

**Repeats:** the Ohio stain line came back word for word to `look at the stain`, `kiss the stain` and `name the stain. his name is gary`. For a joker, a bit that gets the same answer three times is dead.

**Where she nearly quit:** Tuesday 5 at the driveway. She typed two real, in-character attempts in a row. One was told to "say something" right after saying something. The other was told it "isn't something this moment has room for" when it was the exact thing. A joker's patience for being misunderstood is the same as her patience for being understood: low. One more miss and she types `restart` or closes the tab. `don't go. not a bit. seriously` was her giving up on her own voice to get past the parser. It worked, and the scene was worth it, but that's backwards: the sincere line came from parser fatigue, not from the story.

**Confusing:** on the second Tuesday she didn't know the day was special, and the pie input ended it with no warning. She'd never know she lost an ending.

## Takeaway for the author

The game gets through to the joker in exactly one way, and it works every time: **it quotes her own joke back at her.** "She said you made a joke." "Hotel California, but with a Dollar General." "*Chicago was a stupid idea anyway*. She never said that." Each of those cracked the bit more than any scene about Maya did. Where it fails her is the other direction: when *she* jokes, the game either swallows it (her text becomes "> hi.", her pie becomes "you make yourself look at him") or misreads it ("say something" right after she said something, Mom "isn't here"). For this player, the inputs *are* the character. Her jokes are her avoidance, and the strongest thing the game could do is answer them *as avoidance*: let her ring and run, let her type the joke text and delete it, let her pie turn into turning around. Two structural notes. First, the second Tuesday is very fragile for experimenters: she lost it to the word "laughed" without ever knowing it was special. Second, she reached the true ending in 7 Tuesdays without ever looking at the phone, the photos or the sketchbook, and her one sincere line, `don't go. not a bit. seriously`, came from parser fatigue, not from the story. Give the joker a road to her feelings that runs through her own jokes (proposal 6), and her ending would be earned instead of found.
