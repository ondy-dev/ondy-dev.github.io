## Friction

- **"Sorry" breaks texting.** On day 4 at 7:14, `reply to her. im so sorry…` and `text her sorry` both got *"She isn't here. Not yet."* `sorry` sits above `text` in the global word list, so the apology word takes over the message. This is the most likely thing a guilty player types at 7:14 once she knows about the message.
- **A free reply lost to a branch word.** On day 5 at the diner, `order something so he has a reason to stay` played the full Theo branch, because "stay" is a Theo word and branches beat free replies. That cost the day, and the replay was identical with no "you've done this" note.
- **A specific apology flattened.** On day 6 at the driveway, "sorry about Thursday" played "I'm not mad… say bye properly… the small things instead of the big ones." She said the big one.
- **Texting from bed is "gone."** On day 8 at 8:40, `text her have a safe trip` got *"That moment's already gone."* The obvious move from that bed is the phone.
- **"See her off" bought a ticket.** On day 8 at 8:40, she meant a wave. THINK then says she "tried leaving with her." Her third fix was an accident. Without it, she'd have had peace and sorry and nowhere obvious to go.
- **A hug turned into a question.** On day 9 in the kitchen, `hug her. tell her its okay, i already know` played Nell asking "What did Lourdes say?" and Mom saying "I thought you knew."
- **A whisper heard through a door.** On day 10 at the porch, `whisper sorry, so quiet she cant hear it` played as a repeat knock, and she "asks" why it sounds practiced. **This is where she nearly quit.** It's eleven days in, she was doing exactly what the game asked, and one inaudible sorry restarted everything.
- **Day one echo.** `mom are you okay?` and `its okay theo…` were echoed as actions ("You mean to \*mom are you okay\*"). `text her im sorry` at 8:40 got "You sit up. You lie back down."
- Minor: from day 3 on, the prompt line was blank (no "type, or ENTER" reminder), so it wasn't always clear whether ENTER would let a moment pass.

## Proposals

### 1. "Sorry" inside a text is still a text
- **Moment:** 7:14
- **Typed:** `reply to her. im so sorry`, `text her sorry`
- **Needs:** none (the text branch already has `[if read] > sorry. yes. tower at 3?`)
- **Learn:** same as `text`
- **Fix:** peace (unchanged)
- **Sketch:** a data-only change. Add `words: { text: ["reply", "text her", "text maya", "text back", "message her"] }` to the wake moment. Moment words are checked before the global list, so "sorry" can't take over.
- **Failed version:** n/a (this is a parser fix)
- **Why:** a guilty player apologizes *in* the text. Right now the apology is exactly what blocks it.

### 2. Sorry for Thursday, at the driveway
- **Moment:** 6:30 driveway
- **Typed:** `im sorry about thursday`, `i should have listened`
- **Needs:** tried_to_tell
- **Learn:** none
- **Fix:** sorry (a second road to the same fix, so the five-fix table stays intact). Or none, if you'd rather keep sorry tied to the knock.
- **Sketch:** *"I'm sorry. About Thursday. I made a joke because I didn't want you to finish the sentence." / Maya sets the box on the hood. "I know. I let you." / Then she's the one holding you, in her driveway, on the night she's leaving.* Night: *"You went over there to say sorry and she ended up comforting you. ## Takeaway

For a player whose only move is kindness, *Tomorrow* nearly works, and her best moments are when it refuses her. "What comes out is something else" on day one and "sorry doesn't make it un-happen" on day seven are the exact lessons this player needs, and the clear porch line ("meant it the other way too… One of us should") is the forgiveness she was looking for the whole time, given by the honest day itself instead of by Maya. The problem is that her gentleness keeps getting misread. "Sorry" blocks her text. "Stay" (meaning pie) eats a day. "Sorry about Thursday" becomes small talk about the fryer. A hug becomes a question. "See her off" buys a bus ticket. A whisper meant to go unheard counts as a practiced repeat. Her kind inputs collapse into one scene (the peace goodbye) or get turned into someone else's action, so she rarely feels her own way of wanting being answered, and she reached the third fix only by accident. On the second Tuesday she held out against Mom but gave in for Theo, because helping someone doesn't feel like changing anything. If you want this kind of player to have a fair shot at the true-true ending, make sure a poke never hands her someone to comfort for free. If you want her to have her own failure, give the apology that asks to be forgiven its own branch.

*What I didn't do: I didn't replay any day to test alternate inputs, so some proposals (2, 3, 7) are untested sketches. The data-only parser fixes (1, 5, 6, and the diner `order` collision) follow from reading `findAction`/`freeReply` in game.js, which checks moment words first and lets a branch beat a free reply.*
