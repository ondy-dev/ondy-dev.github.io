## Takeaway for the author

The game *hears* anger. "yell", "scream", "confront", "fine", "everybody knew" are all in the word lists. But it never lets anger be *voiced*: every angry input either becomes what Nell already did, gets "Not yet." or "no room for", or is quietly rewritten into a softer girl ("Mom, what did Lourdes say?", "sorry. yes. tower at 3?", "Stay. Please.", "You knock."). For this player, that felt like being corrected by her own narrator for nine Tuesdays. What finally reached her was not a fight but the clear porch line, "meant it the other way too. *Go.* Get out of here. One of us should", because it took her cruel line seriously as hers and found something true inside it. That's the model. Let her say the angry thing, let it fail for its own reason ("she admits it and still goes"), and put a sliver of Maya's half somewhere she can reach. Right now Maya's half lives only in the day-two epilogue, and the angry player is exactly the one who breaks on day two, at the second poke, at the first person she's allowed to blame.
