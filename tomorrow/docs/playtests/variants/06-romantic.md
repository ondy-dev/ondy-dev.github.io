## The second Tuesday

She gave in at the **first moment, 7:14**, and never saw a single in-moment poke.

- The morning poke ("You could do anything today. That's the worst part. You could do anything.") felt like permission, not a warning. *Anything. Okay. I'll tell her. Right now.*
- `text maya i love you` → "She isn't here. Not yet." She read that as *not by text, then, in person*, and was fine with waiting for 6:30.
- `look` → the room, including "A corkboard of photo-booth strips." The help line had told her LOOK doesn't change anything, so `look at the photo strips` felt free. It wasn't: at 7:14, looking at things is a real change (game.js says so; the player can't see that). The day ended on the floor under the corkboard.

Honest counterfactual: even if the look had been free, **she would not have resisted the driveway poke**, which literally hands her the words she came for: "*Stay. I'm sorry. I love you.* Anything." A player like her caves there every time. The "without knowing" ending is not built for her, and that's probably correct: she isn't someone who can live without knowing whether it was mutual. But she should lose the second Tuesday to *that* poke, on purpose, not to a look she thought was free.

## Friction

- **"She isn't here. Not yet." for a text** (T2 and T4, 7:14). Texting is how you reach someone who isn't here. `text maya i love you` hits the confess word "love" before "text" and gets the in-person refusal.
- **`look at the photo strips` ended her second Tuesday.** game.js has a deliberate exception ("Looking at things is free (except at 7:14…)"), but HELP and the miss hint both say LOOK doesn't change anything, and `look at the hook` in the same moment *is* free. The only place the rule bends is the one day that matters most.
- **`climb up and tell her how i feel` at 3:15 replayed the identical tower scene** (third time she'd read it, second time in full) and ate the day. The street's word list maps "her" to the tower. Also, text→tower (T3) and street→tower (T4) are different branch names, so the repeat rule ("Why does it sound like you practiced that?") never fired, even though it was the same scene.
- **The confess-fail told her she'd never looked**, the day after she'd looked at the strip all day, with "since august" in her input. Nearly quit here (T5).
- **THINK never shows the photo or August.** Half a key is invisible, so she had no way to know she was one memory away.
- **The confession hedges her.** She typed "I've loved you since the night the power went out"; Nell says "I think… I don't know. Since the tower." It recovers ("I know."), but the first line is the game's Nell, not hers.
- **`let me come with you`** missed at the porch (and would have at the driveway).
- **The house night "Because what?"** after she already knew because.
- **The honest day told her things she never learned.** Clear diner: "It's at the TV." She never breathed or talked to Theo. For her it was the best line in the game, but it goes against the author's rule (Decision 5) that the honest day shouldn't mention things Nell never found out. Meanwhile the clear morning kept "Maya never replied last night." because she never scrolled up. So the honest narrator still lies to her about the text, and she finished the game never knowing Maya replied.
- **Operator note, not a game bug:** twice (T8, T9) I batched one ENTER too many and passed the driveway. A real player sees each moment, so I don't count it against the game. The prompt hint does go blank after day 2, though, so a fast ENTER-er who knows the day by heart could do the same.

## Proposals

### 1. Say it at the tower
- **Moment:** 3:15 street (and the tower via text at 7:14).
- **Typed:** `climb up and tell her how i feel`
- **Needs:** feelings
- **Learn:** reason, why_secret, tried_to_tell (as the tower already does)
- **Fix:** confess
- **Sketch:** After "You were the only one.": *“I love you,” you say, to the feed store, because you can't say it to her face yet. She stops picking at the thread. “Say it to me.” You do. Up here there's nobody to see but the whole town, and the whole town fits in one hand.*
- **Failed version (no feelings):** the tower scene, then at the ladder: *“Maya, I—” “What?” “You've got charcoal on your thumb.” “I always do.”* Night: *You climbed all the way up there to say it.*
- **Why:** the confess chain itself names the tower ("The night she fell asleep on my shoulder at the tower"), but the tower is the one place you can't say it, and right now "tell her" at 3:15 is swallowed by the moment word "her". Needs street words `confess: [...]` above `tower`, and a confess case in the tower branch.

### 2. Typing it to her phone (free reply)
- **Moment:** 7:14 wake
- **Typed:** `text maya i love you`
- **Needs:** none
- **Learn:** none
- **Fix:** none (free reply, keeps the day)
- **Sketch:** *You type it. i love you. The cursor blinks after it like it's waiting for the rest. You delete it one letter at a time. Not to the back of her phone.*

## Takeaway for the author

For a Nell who already knows she's in love, the feelings lock reads as the game telling her she's in denial. She's not; she's afraid of being seen. "You've never let yourself look" landed as an accusation the day after she'd spent a whole Tuesday under the corkboard and typed "since August". Then `remember august` produced the best scene she played ("Since before the tower"), so the lock is worth keeping as a destination. The failure text needs to acknowledge the half she's already looked at. Her two big frustrations were word routing, not writing: "tell her how I feel" at the tower replayed the sunset, and "text maya i love you" was told "She isn't here." Her second Tuesday was lost to a look she had been told was free. And the game already holds the cure for her real fear: "Nobody is looking at you." Right now she only reaches it by accident on the honest day. Letting that line touch the confession would make the romantic route feel written for her too.
