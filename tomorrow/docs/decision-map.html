TOMORROW

The decision map

This is every path through the game: what the player can do at each of the seven moments, what each choice leads to, and what Nell has to know for some of them to work. I generate it from game/story.js, so it always matches what's actually in the game.

A few words I use throughout. A moment is one of the seven places the day stops and waits. A change is the one thing the player can do differently each day, and it usually ends the day. A fact is something Nell finds out. Facts are the only thing that survives the loop. A fix is a change that tries to repair the day, and the way out needs 3 different ones.

How a turn works

Each time the player presses ENTER, the game goes down this list and stops at the first thing that fits.

  1. RESTART opens the restart menu. "Start the loop over" wipes what Nell knows and wakes her on the second Tuesday, skipping day one, and keeps the endings found and the colors. "Erase everything" clears those too.
  2. A few things are answered anywhere, even at night: thoughts of self-harm get the 988 line, and "make it stop" or "what's the point" get their own replies.
  3. HELP, LOOK, THINK and inventory never use up the day. THINK lists what Nell knows.
  4. An empty ENTER lets the moment happen the way it happened, and the day moves on.
  5. On day one, nothing can change. The game says what Nell meant to do, then it happens the old way.
  6. The game finds the action in what was typed. It checks the moment's own words first, then the global list, and the first match wins.
  7. If it's what Nell actually did ("eat", "keep walking", "fine, then go"), the moment happens.
  8. Anything starting with "look at" is free, at every moment.
  9. On the second Tuesday, hiding in bed gets one warning first.
  10. If the action is a change at this moment, it plays. The branch picks its first case whose facts Nell knows.
  11. If there's a free reply for it, at this moment or anywhere, it answers and the moment stays open.
  12. If it's a change that belongs to another moment, the game says "Not yet." or "That moment's already gone."
  13. Otherwise it's a miss, and after two misses the game gives a hint.

When a change plays, making the same fix twice doesn't count. Nell gets "Why does it sound like you practiced that?" and nothing is learned. If a fact completes the sketchbook, or both the photo strip and August, Nell admits her feelings on her own. Any change on the second Tuesday adds "And just like that, it's a different day."

Each moment is told one of three ways, depending on one fact. Before Nell knows it, the day is told the way she felt it, and reality warps. After she knows it, the truth shows through. On the last day, once the way out is open, it's told the way it really was, and only with what she actually found out.

How the loop ends

At the end of a day where the player let all seven moments happen, the game checks these in order.

  1. Day one always leads to the first night. Tomorrow will be different.
  2. Without Knowing. Once the player has found Knowing and restarted, on the second Tuesday, if Nell changes nothing and learns nothing, the loop ends here. On a first run the same day just loops with You can’t let go of something you’ve never once picked up. She never finds out why, she gets up anyway, and the epilogue jumps ten years later to Dot's Diner. This is the only way to see the epilogue.
  3. Knowing. If Nell has made 3 different fixes and knows why Maya never told her, the day is told honestly and the loop ends. The night the way out opens, the game says Something about the dark feels different tonight. Thinner. On a ready day, watching the bus leave from the tower at 8:40 ends it the same way.
  4. Otherwise the day loops, and the night says what's missing:
    • She hasn't found out anything yet: You can’t let go of something you’ve never once picked up.
    • She knows things but hasn't tried a fix: But you already started pulling at the thread. You can’t stop halfway and call it letting go.
    • She's tried fewer than 3 fixes: But at every one of them, you stopped and looked. There are still things you haven’t said to her. You can feel the shape of them in your mouth.
    • She has the fixes but not the why: But you still don’t know why. She’s up there every afternoon. You’ve never once asked.

The six fixes are peace, keep, confess, sorry, station and permission. The bar at the top of the game counts the endings found, and that count survives a restart.

The seven moments

The day runs top to bottom. At each moment the player can press ENTER or make one change. A change that ends the day sends Nell to 11:52 PM and back to 7:14 the next morning.
