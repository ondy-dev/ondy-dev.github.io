How the game decides

Every time the player presses ENTER, the game checks these in order and stops at the first that fits.

  1. RESTART, anywhere: the restart menu. Back to the first Tuesday erases what Nell learned and keeps the endings found and the colors. Erase everything also erases those and asks how you feel again.
  2. Answers that work anywhere (STORY.anywhere): unsafe, stop, tired. Safety first, in every mode, even at night.
  3. HELP, LOOK, THINK, inventory: free. THINK lists every fact Nell knows.
  4. ENTER on an empty line: the moment happens the way it happened (its letItHappen), and the day moves on. An ENTER within 0.7s of a new page is ignored.
  5. Only punctuation or emoji: not understood.
  6. Day one: nothing can change. The game repeats back what was meant (“You mean to…”), then the moment happens anyway.
  7. Find the action: the moment's own words first, then the global words, top to bottom. The first match wins.
  8. What Nell actually did (nothing, or a change marked “let it happen”): the moment happens.
  9. “look at …” / “look around”: always free. The moment's reply for that thing, or LOOK.
  10. Hiding on the second Tuesday: warned once (“Last Tuesday you didn’t”). The second try hides.
  11. A change: the branch. Its first case whose needs Nell knows plays. See below.
  12. A free reply: the moment's own, then the global replies. The moment stays open.
  13. An action from another moment: “Not yet.” (or “She isn’t here. Not yet.” for Maya) if it comes later, “That moment’s already gone.” if it came earlier.
  14. Anything else: one of the three misses, and from the second miss on, a hint.

When a change plays

  1. The first case whose needs are all known is chosen.
  2. If that case is a fix and this scene was already used for a fix (scene lets text and tower count as one), it's a repeat: “Why does it sound like you practiced that?” and the night says to try something else. Nothing is learned.
  3. Otherwise its text plays and its facts are learned. If that completes sketch, or photo + august, Nell admits her feelings (learns feelings).
  4. No night: it was a reply, and the moment stays open.
  5. On the second Tuesday: “And just like that, it’s a different day.” The next morning remembers how far she got, if it was the street or later.
  6. wayOut: the loop ends here (watching the bus go, on a ready day). Otherwise the fix is recorded and the night begins.

How honestly each moment is told

Each moment has a truth fact. Lines tagged [felt] show before Nell knows it (reality warps), [heal] after she knows it, and [clear] on the honest last day, once the way out is open. [day two] lines (the pushes to change something) show only on the second Tuesday.

The two endings

Knowing

Opens: 3 different fixes out of peace keep confess sorry station permission, and why_secret. The night it opens: “Something about the dark feels different tonight. Thinner.”

Then: let all seven moments happen, told honestly. Or, on a ready day, watch the bus go from the tower at 8:40.

Ends: the last night, Wednesday, “get up”. Some people let go the very next day, without ever finding out.

Without Knowing

Opens: only on the second Tuesday, and only if nothing is learned that day.

Then: resist every push to change something, and let all seven moments happen.

Ends: “You close your eyes, and for the first time in two days, you don’t ask for another Tuesday.” Wednesday, “get up”, then ten years later at Dot’s.

The endings found are counted in the bar at the top and survive RESTART.
