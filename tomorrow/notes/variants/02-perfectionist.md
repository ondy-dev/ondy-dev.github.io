# Nell, variant 02: the perfectionist (anxiety 8/10)

_A real playthrough of the game (story.js + game.js via the harness), one input at a time, as a Nell who believes there is a correct version of the day and that she can find it by planning hard enough. She also stands in for the optimizer/completionist player. Session file: `scratchpad/harness/sessions/nell-02-perfectionist.txt` (86 inputs)._

**Result:** the true ending (with the truth), on the 14th Tuesday. Day two: she resisted the kitchen and diner pokes and gave in at 3:15 (the tower). She never knocked and never ran for the bus.

Blockquotes are Nell. Plain text is for the author. `code` names facts, branches and moments in `story.js`. **NEW** marks things the game doesn't have.

---

## 1. Who I am

> It's 2 AM and I'm rewriting it. Not the whole day, just the driveway. Version eleven: "I'm not mad." No. Version twelve: "I'm proud of you." Better, but I'd say it too fast, I always say the good stuff too fast. I have a list on my phone called *things to say* and it has never once been used for its purpose.
>
> I'm Nell. I redo things. Essays, eyeliner, the order I put the mugs back. If something comes out wrong I don't feel bad, exactly. I feel *unfinished*, like a tab left open.
>
> Today came out wrong. I said four words and I've replayed them, conservatively, two hundred times. *Fine. Then go.* I can hear exactly where my voice went flat. I can hear the half-second where I could have stopped.
>
> There's a right version. There has to be. Somewhere there's a Tuesday where I say it in the right order at the right time with my feet in the right place, and she gets on the bus and it's *fine*, actually fine, not *Fine.* fine.
>
> If I get to do it again I'm not wasting it.

### Trait sheet

| | |
|---|---|
| **Anxiety (8/10), how it shows** | Not panic, mostly. Rehearsal. Her anxiety runs forward as scripts and backward as replays. Physically: chest at the diner like everyone's, but her worst moments are quiet, at night, "running the numbers." She rations her one change like it's the last one. |

## 5. Friction

**Inputs that should have worked and didn't**

1. **`please stay. don't go to chicago`** (driveway, Tuesday 5) → "say what" nudge. The driveway's moment words map `chicago` to `talk`, and moment words are checked before the global `stay`. The single clearest "stay" input a player can type fails. Fix: drop `chicago` from the driveway's `talk` words, or check `stay` first.
2. **`rehearse`, `make a list`** (7:14, Tuesday 2) → generic misses, the second with "That isn't something this moment has room for." Rehearsing is her entire coping mechanism. The game told her it doesn't exist.
3. **`ask her about the fourth frame in the photo strip`** (driveway, Tuesday 8) → "That moment's already gone. Today, anyway." `photo` matched the wake action. She meant the fact, not the moment. The reply reads as nonsense.
4. **`tell her how i feel. i looked this time`** (driveway, Tuesday 8) → the same failure night, "You've never let yourself look", the day after she spent a whole Tuesday looking at the strip. Correct per the rules (`feelings` needs `photo` **and** `august`), but the text doesn't acknowledge she's halfway. **This is where she nearly quit.**
5. **`i love you. not i think. i know`** (driveway, Tuesday 11) → "practiced that." She changed the one word she hated. The repeat ignored the edit, after the previous night promised "Tomorrow you'll get it exactly right."
6. **`go to the tower early and be there first`** (7:14, Tuesday 3) → "practiced that", with a night about "6:31", the driveway, though she'd been on the tower at 3.
7. **Day one, `why didn't you tell me`** → "You mean to *why didn't you tell me*." `whatYouMeant` doesn't treat question words (why/what/when/how/where/can/will) as speech, so it should have been "You mean to say *“Why didn't you tell me.”*"

**Confusing**

- **Repeat rule is per branch, not per scene.** `tower` from the morning on Tuesday 3 was refused as practiced. `text` on Tuesday 13 played the identical tower scene in full. An optimizer reads this as an exploit or a bug.
- **Goodbye after tower.** Same fix (`peace`), so no progress. The only signal was the identical night. She figured it out, but only because she compares nights word for word.
- **The clear day arrives uninvited.** On Tuesday 11 she was ENTERing toward her planned change and suddenly the diner was honest ("It's at the TV. It was always at the TV.") without her ever breathing or talking to Theo. It's lovely, but she felt the diner truth was handed to her rather than found. A completionist notices she never got the `nobody_laughed` scene at all.
- **THINK's "There's one thing you haven't tried"** while knock and the bus were still untried. To a checklist-reader, the list is wrong.

**Near-quit points**

- **Tuesday 8**, the second confess failure after the photo. "The game watched me do it. Now it's saying I didn't." A real perfectionist player alt-tabs to a guide here.
- **Tuesday 11**, the edited confession refused. Less a quit than a slow burn: the game's own nights keep saying "get it right / exactly right" and then punish her for trying to.

- "And some small part of you — mean, or kind, you honestly can't tell — meant it the other way too."
- "You don't tell yourself anything about tomorrow." After thirteen nights of "Tomorrow you'll get it right", the absence is the ending.
- "14 Tuesdays. There were other ways to spend them." Hit hard, and also (see below) reads as a score.

**Rang false**

- "*She said she tried.* You can't stop hearing Theo say it." (`phone`). She learned it from Maya on the tower and never heard Theo say it.
- "There's one thing you haven't tried." (THINK, `ready`). She hadn't knocked or run for the bus.
- "You've told her everything." (THINK, `confess`). She told her one thing.
- "You've given her a perfect goodbye." (THINK, `peace`). The game's own checklist calls it *perfect* while the night calls it a photograph. For this player, the word "perfect" in the tracker undercuts the whole point.
- “Why didn't you tell me?” on the tower on Tuesday 13, when she has known `why_secret` for eleven Tuesdays.
- "If you'd said it at 6:31." after a repeat at the tower at 3 PM.
- “I think I'm in love with you” when she typed "i love you." The scene earns it two lines later, but it put a hedge in the mouth of the one player who'd edited the hedge out.
- "Tomorrow you'll get it right" / "exactly right" (PEACE_NIGHT, confess night). Not false in voice, but for a perfectionist these are promises, and she took them literally.

## 8. Takeaway for the author

For a perfectionist, the game's own voice is the strongest enemy of its theme: nights promise "Tomorrow you'll get it right" and "exactly right", THINK calls the goodbye "perfect" and works as a checklist, and the end card's "14 Tuesdays" reads as a score she immediately wanted to beat.
