## Takeaway for the author

The knowledge chain holds. A player who reads closely and LOOKs at everything can go Mom → Theo → phone → Mrs. Reyes → text → tower and reach the true ending in 12 Tuesdays without guessing. The 11:52 timestamp on every night is the best hidden clue in the game. The porch `window` reply let her solve the knock fix by inference, which is the most "detective" moment in the game. What hurts this player is the interface, not the story. A LOOK at 7:14 silently ended her one chance at the without-knowing ending. Rereading a known text costs a whole day. Asking Mom a sharper question replays the old scene and replaces her words. THINK drops three of the clues she found. Fix those four (free reread, a warning on 7:14 looks, a Mom follow-up, a fuller THINK) and add one line of evidence (the letter's date, or how Theo knew about Thursday), and the investigator's run becomes the deduction game she thought she was playing. Without them, a quarter of her Tuesdays (T2, T6, T10) went to traps and reruns.
